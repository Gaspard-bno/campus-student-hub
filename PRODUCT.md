# PRODUCT.md — Campus Student Hub

## Product purpose
Campus Student Hub is a conceptual interactive prototype of a mobile web app that centralizes access to campus administrative and support services (academic advising, counselling, financial aid, housing, international office, health) in one place, in English, Portuguese and French, available 24/7. It was designed by Crousty Studio for Project 4 (Digital Innovation & Experimentation) of the FAE Creativity & Innovation course. Its job in the course is to let a student **attempt the main task without explanation** during an exploratory usability test, and to be demonstrated live in class.

It is not a working MVP. It does not connect to any real university system. All services, advisors and time slots are demo data.

## Users
- All university students. Primary persona: a **newly arrived international student** in the first hectic weeks of the semester, on a smartphone, between classes, often in a second or third language, unsure which office handles what.
- Secondary: test participants (classmates) and the professor watching the demo on a projector.

## Problem (from the team's Canva, Step 1)
International students struggle to quickly locate and access essential campus services during their first weeks, because information is scattered across offices and websites and language barriers add friction. Today they ask staff at various desks, make phone calls, or navigate complex decentralized university websites.

## Main hypothesis (Step 1B)
We believe that international university students will be able to identify and book the correct campus service using our interface without assistance, because the app clearly categorizes services and simplifies navigation.

## Main task to support
Find the counselling support service and complete an appointment booking.

## Brand personality
Warm, reassuring, direct. "We're here to help." Calm confidence rather than corporate cheerfulness. The visual world is Crousty Studio's: deep near-black surfaces lit by a soft orange-pink-violet aurora glow, technical monospace details, a confident grotesque for headings.

## Tone of voice
Short sentences, plain words, second person, no jargon, no exclamation marks in system copy, no em dashes. Name the next action. Never blame the user.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboard templates (hero metric cards, identical icon grids with three lines of text).
- Bureaucratic university portals with dense link lists.
- Purple/white "default student app" look (the team's first AI-generated screens used it; the team chose to move to the Crousty dark identity).
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, neon-on-black crypto aesthetics.

## Strategic principles
1. The main task must be completable in under one minute by a first-time user, without help.
2. Everything simulated is labelled as simulated.
3. Language switching is instant and complete (no half-translated screens).
4. The demo must work offline from a local file.
